How it works

Two minutes, no jargon.

You give it a Vinted search — the address bar after you set your filters. Every few minutes it asks Vinted for that search’s newest listings, exactly as a browser would, without logging in. Anything new that passes your filters is sent to your destinations (Telegram, Discord, ntfy, a webhook). The first check of a new search only records what is already there, so you are not flooded.

Vinted does not like being asked too often. The app paces itself (at most {{ settings_view.site_requests_per_minute }} requests a minute per country site, across all searches), and when Vinted refuses a request every search on that site backs off together — you will see cooling in the table and get a notice if a destination has health notices on. That is deliberate, not a fault.

Words on the Searches page

Total price
What you actually pay: the asking price plus Vinted’s buyer-protection fee. All price filters and alerts use the total. Vinted’s own price filter does not.
Only the cheapest … %
Every check sees up to 96 listings; all of them are remembered for 30 days with their price, filters or not. When a new listing arrives, we count how many remembered ones are cheaper. Set it to 25 and you only hear about listings in the cheapest quarter of the current market. It follows the season by itself — no need to guess a price. It needs 10 remembered listings before it starts filtering, so on a niche search it may take a few days. The alert says it in words: “cheaper than 88 % of 312 similar listings seen this month” — the higher, the better.
Check every
Seconds between checks. Vinted’s own listing feed lags by minutes, so checking faster than every 2 minutes finds nothing sooner and gets the address blocked. 120–600 is sensible; the “Apply to all” box changes every search at once.
States
ok normal · starting no check finished yet · cooling Vinted refused a request on this site and all searches on it are waiting · stale no new listing for far longer than this search usually goes (only when its page is full and other searches on the site keep moving) · failing the last check errored, the reason is under the name · paused you paused it.
Newest listing
When the newest listing Vinted returned was posted. A healthy busy search moves this every few minutes. A niche search may not move it for days — that is fine.
Found
How many listings this search has recorded, including the first-check seed.
Clone
Runs the same search, filters and destinations on another country site. Vinted lets you buy across EU sites, and .de, .pl or .cz have far more listings than .sk. A listing that shows on several sites is only alerted once.
Pause vs Delete
Pause stops checking and keeps everything. Delete removes the search, its recorded listings and its market memory.

What an alert contains

📊 cheaper than … %
The market position explained above.
📉 Price drop
A listing you already saw got at least {{ settings_view.price_drop_min_percent }} % cheaper. Costs no extra requests: the listing was on the page anyway.
🔥 HOT DEAL · 🤖 · 💤
Only if an AI agent is connected (below). 🔥 the agent scored it {{ settings_view.enrichment_highlight_score }} or more; 🤖 a normal verdict; 💤 scored under {{ settings_view.enrichment_silent_below }} or “not the product you searched for” — delivered without a notification sound.
Skip seller
Adds the seller to that search’s skip list. Undo it in the search’s Edit form.
Pause search
Same as the Pause button here. /resume <id> in Telegram brings it back; ids are in /status.
👍 / 👎
Tells the agent whether it called this one right. Your last few answers on a search are shown to it next time, so it learns what you call a deal.
Quiet hours
Per destination. Nothing is sent inside the window; what turned up arrives together when it ends (Telegram folds more than five into one message). Times are read in {{ settings_view.timezone }}.
Health notices
Warnings and the Monday summary go only to destinations with this switched on. Have at least one.

The AI agent (optional)

Add a plain webhook destination pointing at your agent (the reference is an n8n workflow with an LLM) and route searches to it alongside Telegram. Each new listing goes to the agent at once with its photos, the market position, retail prices found earlier and your 👍/👎 history; the chat alert waits up to {{ settings_view.enrichment_wait_s }} s ({{ 'off — set ENRICHMENT_WAIT_S to turn on' if not settings_view.enrichment_wait_s else 'ENRICHMENT_WAIT_S' }}) for the agent to post back a verdict: what product it really is, what it costs new, whether it matches your search, any authenticity worries, a deal score and one sentence. The verdict is woven into the alert. If the agent is silent, the alert goes out as usual — you never lose one.

Contract for building your own agent: docs/enrichment.md in the repository.
