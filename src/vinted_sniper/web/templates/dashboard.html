How the filters work together

A listing has to pass every filter you set. Vinted’s own filters (brand, size, category, price) are in the URL and Vinted applies them before we see anything. Ours run on what comes back: the total-price cap, the “cheapest … %” market cap, title words, and seller checks.

“Only the cheapest … %” is the one worth understanding. Every check, this search sees up to 96 listings; all of them are remembered for 30 days with their price. When a new listing appears, we count how many remembered ones are cheaper. “25” means: alert only if fewer than 25 % of them are cheaper — i.e. it is in the cheapest quarter of the current market. Unlike a fixed price cap, this follows the season and the market by itself. The alert also tells you, in words: “cheaper than 88 % of 312 similar listings seen this month”.

What a Telegram alert looks like, and what the buttons do
  • Top line — “🔥 HOT DEAL”, “🤖 …” or “💤 …” appears only when an AI agent judged the listing (optional, see Help). 💤 also means the phone will not buzz.
  • 📊 cheaper than 88 % of 312 similar listings — where the price sits among what this search has seen in the last 30 days. Higher is better.
  • 📉 Price drop — a listing you already saw got cheaper.
  • Open listing / Seller profile — links to Vinted.
  • Skip seller — never show this seller again for this search. Pause search — stop it until you resume (here or with /resume).
  • 👍 / 👎 — tell the agent whether it called this one right; it learns your taste.
